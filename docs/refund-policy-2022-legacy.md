# Refund Policy (Legacy — 2022 revision, superseded)

Note: this document describes our old policy and is retained for historical
reference only. It should no longer be used to answer customer questions.

Under the 2022 policy, annual plans were eligible for a refund within
**90 days of purchase**, provided account usage was under 20% of the monthly
quota. Monthly plans were non-refundable under this version of the policy.

This policy was replaced in 2025. See refund-policy.md for the current,
correct terms.
