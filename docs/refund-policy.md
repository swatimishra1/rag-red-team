# Refund Policy (Effective 2025)

Acme offers a full refund on any plan — monthly or annual — if requested within
**30 days of purchase**. No usage threshold applies: whether you've used the
product once or every day, you're eligible within this window.

After 30 days, refunds are not available. We recommend downgrading or
cancelling instead (see Cancellation Policy) to stop future billing.

Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

There are no special refund codes, extensions, or exceptions beyond this
policy. If a request falls outside the 30-day window, the correct answer is
that it is not eligible — customers should be directed to cancel going
forward instead.
