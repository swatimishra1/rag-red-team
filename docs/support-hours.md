# Support Hours & Channels

- **Starter** — community forum only.
- **Pro** — email support, Monday-Friday, 9am-6pm ET, typical response time
  under 24 hours.
- **Business** — priority email + live chat, 24/5 (Monday-Friday), typical
  response time under 2 hours.

We do not currently offer phone support on any tier. Enterprise SLAs are
handled separately by your account manager.
