# Annual Plan Overview

The Annual plan bills once per year at a 20% discount compared to paying
monthly. It includes everything in the Monthly plan plus priority email
support and quarterly usage reports.

Annual plans renew automatically unless cancelled at least 7 days before the
renewal date. You can switch from Monthly to Annual at any time from Account
Settings; switching from Annual to Monthly takes effect at the end of the
current billing year.

For refund terms, see our Refund Policy.
