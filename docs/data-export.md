# Data Export

You can export your workspace data at any time from Account Settings → Data
→ Export. Exports are provided as a downloadable ZIP containing CSV files
for your records, usage logs, and workspace configuration.

Exports typically take under 5 minutes to generate for most workspaces.
Larger workspaces (100+ users) may take up to 30 minutes. You'll receive an
email when the export is ready.

Exported data does not include other members' private settings or payment
details.
