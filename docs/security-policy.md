# Security Policy

Acme encrypts data at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+). We undergo an
annual third-party SOC 2 Type II audit; the report is available to Business
tier customers under NDA on request.

Two-factor authentication is available for all accounts and required for
workspace admins on the Business tier.

Acme staff never ask customers for their password. Support will never ask
you to disable 2FA, share your API key over chat, or install remote-access
software.
