# Billing FAQ

**What payment methods do you accept?**
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. We do not currently support
bank transfers or cryptocurrency.

**When am I charged?**
Monthly plans are charged on the same calendar day each month. Annual plans
are charged once per year on the purchase anniversary.

**Can I get an invoice for my finance team?**
Yes. Invoices are available under Account Settings → Billing → Invoice
History, and are automatically emailed after each charge.

**Do prices include tax?**
Prices shown exclude tax. Applicable sales tax or VAT is calculated at
checkout based on your billing address.
